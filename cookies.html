Our cookie practice

Necessary, transparent, and easy to manage.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember your preferences and improve your experience.

2. How we use cookies

We use only strictly necessary cookies to ensure the website functions properly — for example, to keep you logged in or remember your cookie consent choice. We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies.

3. Your control

  • You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.
  • Blocking essential cookies may affect the functionality of the site.
  • We do not share cookie data with third parties.

For more information, visit our Privacy Policy.

4. Changes to this policy

We may update this cookie policy to reflect changes in law or our practices. The latest version will always be available here.

By continuing to use our site, you agree to our use of necessary cookies.

✧ Minimal & essential

We only set cookies that are required for basic site operation. No tracking, no profiling.
