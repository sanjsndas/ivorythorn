Our privacy practice

Rooted in respect, grown with care.

1. Information we gather

We collect only what you choose to share: your name, email address, and any message you send through our contact form. We do not use tracking cookies or analytics that harvest behavioral data.

2. How we use it

Your data helps us respond to your inquiries, send occasional newsletters (only if you opt in), and improve our journal. We never sell or rent your information to third parties.

3. Data storage & security

All correspondence is kept within our private, encrypted garden. We retain messages for as long as needed to serve you, and delete them upon request.

4. Your rights

  • Access, correct, or delete your data at any time.
  • Withdraw consent for marketing communications.
  • Request a copy of the data we hold about you.

To exercise these rights, simply send us a note.

5. Children’s privacy

We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently done so, please contact us immediately.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our practice or for legal reasons. The latest version will always be posted here.

This policy is part of our commitment to the Leafhaven Circle — a place where trust grows as slowly as an old oak.

✧ Your data, your ground

We act as data controller for the information you provide. Our approach is minimal and intentional.
