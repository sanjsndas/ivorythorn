You are welcome to share excerpts for non-commercial purposes, provided you credit the source and link back to the original. We believe in open knowledge, but also in the care of original work.

  • Personal use: You may print, save, or share pages for personal reference.
  • Commercial use: Requires explicit written permission from the Circle.
  • Derivative work: Please ask before adapting or republishing.

2. Community conduct

Our garden is a refuge for thoughtful dialogue. We welcome diverse perspectives, but we do not tolerate harassment, hate speech, or aggressive behavior. Comments, forum posts, and contributions are moderated to maintain a respectful environment.

  • Be kind and constructive.
  • Stay on topic — the earth is wide, but we try to keep the conversation rooted.
